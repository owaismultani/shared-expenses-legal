Delete your Shared account

Shared (com.shared.expenses)

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open Shared and go to Your account (the Account tab on phones).
  2. Under Sync across devices, tap Account, sync and sign out.
  3. Tap Delete cloud account and confirm.

Deletion is immediate and permanent.

Without the app

Email shared@owaism.xyz from the address you signed up with and ask us to delete your account. We complete requests within 30 days and confirm by email.

Delete some of your data without deleting your account

  • An expense or payment: open it and tap Delete. In a shared group the deletion syncs to every member.
  • A whole shared group (if you created it): Your account → Sync across devices → Account, sync and sign out → Delete cloud group. This removes the group, its expenses and its shared receipt files from the cloud for everyone.
  • Everything on this phone: Your account → Erase all data on this device.
  • Anything else: email shared@owaism.xyz from your account’s address and say what you want removed. We complete requests within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your sign-in account (email and password hash) and all active sessions.
  • Every group you own in the cloud, including all its expenses, payments, comments and shared receipt files.
  • Your memberships in groups owned by other people.

What is kept

  • Expenses and payments involving you in groups owned by other people stay in those groups’ history for the remaining members, because they affect everyone’s balances. Your account link to them is removed.
  • Data stored only on your phone is not affected by deleting the cloud account. Remove it from Your account → Erase all data on this device, or uninstall the app.

Deleted cloud data can remain in our database provider’s automatic backups for a short time until those backups expire; it is not restored or used.
