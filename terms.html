Shared helps you record shared expenses and see who owes whom. It is a record-keeping tool. It does not hold, send or receive money, and balances shown are only as accurate as what you and your group members enter.

Content you share

When you share a group, the people you invite can see and edit its expenses, payments, comments, notes and shared receipts. Only invite people you trust, and only add other people’s names or contact details if you have a reason to.
